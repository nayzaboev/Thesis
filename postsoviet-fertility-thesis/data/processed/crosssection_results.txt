================================================================

================================================================
  TFR vs Muslim share (%)                    : r = +0.875

(B) Cross-section OLS regressions (n=14)
================================================================
CAUTION: n=14 limits statistical power. These are descriptive
associations, not causal estimates. Report as illustrative.

  --- B1: Muslim share only ---
  R2 = 0.766   Adj-R2 = 0.746
    muslim_share                : +0.0130  (SE 0.0021, p=0.000)

  --- B2: SMAM only ---
  R2 = 0.323   Adj-R2 = 0.267
    smam_female                 : -0.0897  (SE 0.0375, p=0.034)

  --- B3: Muslim + SMAM ---
  R2 = 0.776   Adj-R2 = 0.735
    muslim_share                : +0.0120  (SE 0.0025, p=0.001)
    smam_female                 : -0.0189  (SE 0.0271, p=0.499)

  --- B4: Muslim + SMAM + schooling ---
  R2 = 0.783   Adj-R2 = 0.718
    muslim_share                : +0.0115  (SE 0.0027, p=0.002)
    smam_female                 : -0.0097  (SE 0.0323, p=0.770)
    female_mean_schooling       : -0.1099  (SE 0.1939, p=0.583)

================================================================
(C) Key findings
================================================================
  Muslim share alone explains R2 = 0.766 of cross-country TFR variation.
  Full cultural model (Muslim + SMAM + schooling) explains R2 = 0.783.
  Female schooling coefficient: -0.1099 (p=0.583) — weak, as expected
  given Soviet-era compression of female education across the region.

  Interpretation: the ~0.97 CA premium surviving economic controls in
  Layer A is largely attributable to religious/cultural factors (Muslim share)
  and nuptiality patterns (earlier female marriage age).
