(B) Hausman test — fixed effects vs random effects
================================================================
  Hausman chi2(4) = 15.18   p = 0.0044
  => Reject H0: random effects is inconsistent. FIXED EFFECTS preferred.
  (We use FE on substantive grounds regardless: strong country heterogeneity,
   and the research question concerns between-country differences handled in Layer A.)

================================================================
(C) Few-cluster caveat
================================================================
  Standard errors throughout Part 2 are clustered on country (14 clusters).
  14 clusters is below the commonly cited safe threshold (~30-50). With few
  clusters, cluster-robust SEs can be anti-conservative (too small), so
  p-values near 0.05 should be read with caution. Recommended robustness
  follow-up: wild-cluster bootstrap (Stata 'boottest', R 'fwildclusterboot').
  This is a known small-N limitation of the post-Soviet sample, not a coding issue.
