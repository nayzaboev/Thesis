(B) Mundlak test — fixed effects vs random effects
================================================================
Classical Hausman is NOT reported: Var(b_FE) - Var(b_RE) is not positive
definite in this sample (3 of 4 eigenvalues negative), so the statistic
is undefined. The Mundlak auxiliary-regression test is used instead.

  H0: all between-country mean coefficients = 0 (random effects consistent)
  F(4, 13) = 21.225   p = 0.0000
  => Reject H0: unobserved country effects are correlated with the
     regressors. Random effects is inconsistent; the between/within
     separation used in Layer A (M2h) and Layer B is required.

================================================================
(C) Few-cluster caveat
================================================================
  Standard errors throughout Part 2 are clustered on country (14 clusters).
  14 clusters is below the commonly cited safe threshold (~30-50). With few
  clusters, cluster-robust SEs can be anti-conservative (too small), so
  p-values near 0.05 should be read with caution. Recommended robustness
  follow-up: wild-cluster bootstrap (Stata 'boottest', R 'fwildclusterboot').
  This is a known small-N limitation of the post-Soviet sample, not a coding issue.
