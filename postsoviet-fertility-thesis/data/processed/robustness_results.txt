  Observed gap: +1.324

(F) INTERACTION FRAGILITY — CA x remittances
======================================================================
Is the CA x remittances interaction driven by particular countries?

  Drop none                      : CA x rem = +0.0157  p=0.001
  Drop Tajikistan                : CA x rem = +0.0143  p=0.008
  Drop Kyrgyzstan                : CA x rem = +0.0164  p=0.002
  Drop Tajikistan + Kyrgyzstan   : CA x rem = +0.0265  p=0.268

  HONEST READING: the interaction survives dropping Tajikistan OR Kyrgyzstan
  individually, but collapses when BOTH are removed. It therefore rests on
  two of the four Central Asian countries and is concentrated in the two
  high-remittance Central Asian economies (Tajikistan and Kyrgyzstan) and
  should NOT be reported as a general Central Asian regularity.
  Remittances are endogenous and do not isolate a migration channel; any
  labour-migration interpretation must be carried by the literature, not
  this regression.

  Note also that all 8 missing remittance observations fall in Central Asia
  (Uzbekistan 5, Tajikistan 2, Kyrgyzstan 1), so remittance coverage is
  weakest precisely in the group of interest.

(G) WILD-CLUSTER BOOTSTRAP — few-cluster robustness check
======================================================================
Rademacher wild-cluster bootstrap (cluster = country, 14 clusters).
With only 14 clusters and 4 treated (Central Asia), asymptotic clustered
p-values are too optimistic. The bootstrap resamples cluster-level weights
and re-estimates, giving a p-value that reduces reliance on conventional cluster asymptotics.
B = 1999 replications. Reference: Cameron, Gelbach & Miller (2008).

  M1 raw CA premium           : coef=+1.3144  p(asymp)=0.000  p(wild-boot)=0.001
  M2 controlled CA premium    : coef=+0.9707  p(asymp)=0.000  p(wild-boot)=0.006
  M2h Mundlak CA premium      : coef=+0.9637  p(asymp)=0.000  p(wild-boot)=0.033
  CA x remittances            : coef=+0.0157  p(asymp)=0.001  p(wild-boot)=0.026
  CA x urbanisation           : coef=-0.0119  p(asymp)=0.054  p(wild-boot)=0.313

  READING: the raw and controlled CA premium remain significant under the
  wild-cluster bootstrap. The interaction terms are much weaker once
  few-cluster inference is used: CA x urbanisation is clearly insignificant,
  and CA x remittances is at best marginal. Report the CA premium as robust
  and the interactions as suggestive only.
  CAVEAT: with only 4 treated clusters, even the wild bootstrap is
  approximate; treat interaction p-values as indicative, not definitive.

  Exact Rademacher enumeration (M2h 'ca' coefficient, 2^14 = 16,384 sign
  assignments — no Monte Carlo error):
    t_obs = +6.873   exact p = 0.0329  (enumerated all 16,384 sign patterns)
    Seeded 1999-draw Monte Carlo wild-cluster bootstrap (above): p = 0.033
    The exact and simulated p-values agree closely, confirming B=1999 draws
    were already sufficient and there is no residual simulation error in
    the headline M2h wild-cluster bootstrap result.
