  INTERPRETATION OF LAYER B:
  The levels-based FE model (A) is reported for transparency but should
  not be treated as the primary within-country estimate. The first-
  difference models (B1/B2) remove the near-integrated component. However,
  the FD results are themselves sensitive to whether year effects are
  included (see B1 vs B2). Therefore, Layer B as a whole is best treated
  as a robustness exercise confirming that within-country economic effects
  are small and fragile — consistent with Layer A's finding that the
  fertility gap is structural and between-country, not driven by year-to-
  year economic fluctuations within individual countries.
