======================================================================
(C2) Descriptive residual persistence check — AR(1) on FE residuals
======================================================================
IMPORTANT: this is a DESCRIPTIVE AR(1) check on the fixed-effects residuals.
It is NOT the Wooldridge-Drukker panel serial-correlation test. A high AR(1)
coefficient indicates the FE-in-levels residuals are persistent, but it does
NOT establish that the residuals contain a unit root, that TFR is I(1), or
that the levels regression is spurious.

  AR(1) coefficient on lagged residual: +0.948 (p=0.000)
  The FE-in-levels residuals are strongly persistent. Because of this
  persistence, the first-difference models (B1/B2) are the more cautious
  within-country specification and the levels FE model (A) should be read
  as descriptive rather than as the primary within-country estimate.

  INTERPRETATION OF LAYER B:
  Within the available annual panel, the associations between TFR and the
  four selected macroeconomic indicators are generally imprecise and
  sensitive to the treatment of common year effects (see B1 vs B2). Layer B
  is therefore best read as a robustness exercise showing that these
  particular within-country economic associations are small and fragile in
  this sample. This does NOT establish that economic factors in general have
  small effects, and it does not rule out other economic, policy or
  demographic mechanisms. Consistent with Layer A, most of the Central
  Asia-rest fertility difference is between-country rather than year-to-year
  within-country variation, but the present design cannot identify the
  mechanisms behind that between-country difference.
