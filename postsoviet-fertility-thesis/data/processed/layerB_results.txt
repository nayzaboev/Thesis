(C2) Wooldridge-style AR(1) serial-correlation check on FE residuals
======================================================================
  AR(1) coefficient on lagged residual: +0.948 (p=0.000)
  An AR(1) coefficient this close to 1 indicates the FE-in-levels residuals
  are essentially non-stationary — consistent with the IPS finding that TFR
  in levels is I(1) but TFR in first differences is stationary (see C1). The
  levels-based FE model is therefore reported for transparency but should not
  be treated as the primary within-country estimate. The FIRST-DIFFERENCE model
  (B) is the more credible within-country estimator here, since differencing
  removes the near-integrated component that clustered standard errors alone
  cannot correct. This is a robustness limitation, not a specification error:
  both estimators point in the same direction on the covariates that matter
  (mortality negative and significant under FD; other economic controls small
  and insignificant in both).
